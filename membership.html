COMMON INQUIRIES

MEMBERSHIP PROTOCOL FAQ

Are there long-term contracts or cancellation penalties?

No. Monthly memberships are billed on a 30-day recurring cadence. You may pause, adjust, or cancel your membership with a simple 30-day written notice via the mobile portal or front desk concierge. Annual memberships are prepaid for 12 months at a 20% discount and are non-refundable after a 14-day grace period.

How does the overcrowding cap operate?

We calculate our active member cap based on our 45,000 square foot floor layout and platform ratios. Once active enrollment reaches capacity, incoming candidates enter a waitlist. This guarantees you will never wait in line for an Olympic rack, deadlift platform, or recovery tank during peak hours.

How does 24/7 biometric gate entry work?

During your onboarding session, our staff scans your biometric profile into our encrypted, locally isolated access security system. You simply approach the high-speed optical turnstile or hold your mobile pass up to bypass into the facility at any hour of the day or night.

Can I freeze my membership if traveling or injured?

Yes. Obsidian and Black Matter tier members can freeze their membership up to 60 days per calendar year at zero administrative cost. Initiate tier members can pause for up to 30 days per calendar year.

What is included in the guest privileges?

Obsidian members receive 2 guest passes each calendar month. Black Matter VIPs receive unlimited guest invitations (one guest per training session). Guests enjoy full access to the training floor and thermal suites alongside the host member.
